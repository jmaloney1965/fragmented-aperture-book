## Push / Sandbox Gotchas Logged Today
- The harness's macOS sandbox blocks the `osxkeychain` credential helper from inside Bash invocations (`-60008 errSecMissingEntitlement`). Workaround: `dangerouslyDisableSandbox: true` on Bash calls that need to push.
- The harness also restricts filesystem writes to specific allowed roots — `/Users/jim.maloney/Codes` is NOT in the allowlist (`Book`, `Projects`, `Work`, `$TMPDIR` are). So `git add` in `~/Codes/openMATCH` failed with "Operation not permitted" on `.git/index.lock` until the sandbox was disabled for that command.
- Background pushes can complete cleanly but take longer than the monitor polling window; if a push appears to hang and a later attempt fails with "already up to date" / "fetch first", the original probably succeeded silently — check `git ls-remote origin` to confirm.

## Lessons for Future Sessions
- After any merge from the M4, immediately check for short-caption gaps if the merge added new figures — the M4 reliably authors long captions only. (Possibly worth a pre-commit hook on the M4 to nudge.)
- After any merge from the M4, verify that referenced image files are present, not just the .tex. Image-PDF commits are easy to miss when authoring on a separate machine.
